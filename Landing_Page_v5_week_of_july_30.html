Saturn Dynamics
Serial Founder PhD Founder AI Native Early Bet
Reservation Deadline
Thu July 30 · 12:00pm ET
Full Deal Room →

Elevator Pitch: Saturn Dynamics is building the world model layer for physical AI: the simulation infrastructure that lets robots, drones, and autonomous systems safely rehearse how to act in the real world before they actually do it, the way a flight simulator trains a pilot before they ever fly a plane. Its core product, Atlas, is a sub-2B parameter world model that outperforms models 7 to 10 times its size from Nvidia, Alibaba, and other leading labs on key benchmarks, while running 30 to 150 times faster and at a fraction of the cost per simulation rollout. Atlas is deployed across 200+ robots with 12 design partners — including Stellantis, Pirelli, and Accenture's Global Robotics Division — with $1M in committed revenue today, on a burn rate of under $150K per month with a team of five. Saturn is raising an $18M seed round (SAFE) at a $120M post-money cap, with $14M+ already committed and NVIDIA Ventures leading.

Founders Elisa Seghetti, Co-Founder & CEO — a repeat founder who started her first AI company, AiGot, at 20 and scaled it to 35+ people, securing NASDAQ-listed enterprise customers including Incyte Bioscience (~$14B pharma); she also ran a board-appointed turnaround to a profitable exit. A Sigma Squared Society Fellow (the invitation-only global community for top entrepreneurs under 26). Simone Totaro, Co-Founder & CTO — a PhD candidate in reinforcement learning at Mila, the Quebec AI institute founded by Yoshua Bengio (Bengio is a Turing Award winner and one of the three “godfathers of deep learning”), with work published at AISTATS 2022 and a research internship at Meta Reality Labs, followed by three years as a Research Scientist at EquiLibre Technologies. Cesc Cunillera, Co-Founder & CSO — a String Theory PhD from the University of Nottingham (Springer PhD thesis prize) with a research fellowship at the Max Planck Institute for Physics; leads the core ML innovations behind Atlas's efficiency advantage.
AV's Access
The current round is Saturn’s first institutional seed round: an $18M SAFE at a $120M post-money cap, with $14M+ already committed. This follows a $3M pre-seed closed in December 2025 from UK/London-based investors, including Sequel, Twin Path Ventures, and angel John Spindler (well-connected into DeepMind’s Genie/world model team). Existing pre-seed investors are following on pro rata alongside new participants: NVIDIA Ventures ($5M), Partech ($2M), Mana Ventures, and a group of technical angels. As a UK-founded company whose cap table has so far been largely European, Saturn is deliberately using this round to build relationships with US funds ahead of its next raise; a priced round expected at a materially higher valuation.
Why Invest in Saturn Dynamics
  • Category-defining efficiency backed by real production traction: While competitors like Nvidia Cosmos, Alibaba Wan, and DeepMind Genie require 10B to 15B parameter models running on multi-GPU H100 clusters, Saturn’s Atlas achieves the same or better benchmark performance at under 2B parameters, running 30 to 150 times faster and at a fraction of the cost per rollout ($0.005 versus $0.037 for Cosmos and $1.10 for Alibaba Wan). Saturn has 200+ robots live in production across 12 design partners — including Stellantis (~$16B market cap), Pirelli (~€7B market cap), and Accenture’s Global Robotics Division (~$180B market cap) — with $1M in committed revenue today, all on a burn rate of under $150K per month with a team of five.
  • An attractive entry point relative to category comparables: Saturn is raising at a $120M post-money cap while direct or adjacent category peers are being priced dramatically higher with less commercial validation — AMI Labs (Yann LeCun) at a $3.5B valuation, World Labs (Fei-Fei Li) at $1B+, Odyssey at a $1.45B valuation, and General Intuition at a $2B pre-money valuation against roughly $2M ARR. NVIDIA Ventures’ participation reflects both technical conviction and a hardware-alignment incentive, as Atlas runs on Nvidia edge silicon.
  • European origination with a deliberate bridge to US capital: Saturn is a UK-based company built by a European technical team (including alumni of Yoshua Bengio’s lab), at a moment when Europe is emerging as a genuine hub for physical AI and world models — evidenced by Yann LeCun basing his new frontier lab in Paris. With capital heavily concentrated in a crowded US AI ecosystem, a position here expands reach into a fast-rising market — a rare, discounted entry into a European deep-tech team before its valuation catches up to US peers.
Key Issues
  • Competitive crowding from dramatically funded labs: The world model category has exploded with capital in 2026 — AMI Labs (Yann LeCun) raised $1.03B, World Labs (Fei-Fei Li) raised $1B, Odyssey raised $310M at a $1.45B valuation, and Prometheus reportedly closed a $12B Series B. Saturn’s efficiency thesis is a genuine technical edge today, but it is not a structural moat that prevents well-capitalized competitors from closing the gap.
  • Small team executing against a broad roadmap: The company is five people (three founders, one ops/finance hire, one research fellow) executing against a wide scope — three model lines, a developer SDK/API platform, and two distinct go-to-market motions. Hiring execution post-raise will be critical to avoid stretching thin.
  • Regulatory uncertainty in core European markets: The EU Machinery Regulation’s “substantial modification” concept could classify certain model updates as safety-relevant, potentially triggering new conformity assessments and making the deploying entity liable as a manufacturer. Given Saturn’s customer base is concentrated in European industrial and automotive OEMs (Stellantis, Pirelli, Iveco, Leonardo), this is a live regulatory risk.

Tiny Health
Serial Founder Patent Holder Scaling Now Deep Science
Reservation Deadline
Thu July 30 · 12:00pm ET
Full Deal Room →

Elevator Pitch: Tiny Health creates microbiome testing kits and personalized plans for the 75% of Americans that suffer from chronic conditions — many of which trace back to the gut. It pairs an in-house patented AI analysis with the world’s largest consumer microbiome database of 120,000+ sequences and 110,000 longitudinal profiles. Paid partnerships span Mayo Clinic, Kaiser, Function Health, and Whoop. 2025 revenue doubled to $12M (103% year-over-year) across three streams: D2C ($399/yr membership), healthcare providers (6,800+ practitioners), and B2B (37 partnerships) — and the company is poised to surpass $28M in 2026 revenue ($53M if pilot partnerships convert). It is now raising a $30M Series B led by B Capital ($20M check) at a $150M pre-money valuation, with Series A lead Spero Ventures participating pro-rata.

Founders Cheryl Sew Hoy, CEO (Cornell B.S./M.S. Engineering) — a second-time founder who built and sold Reclip.It, a personalized shopping and digital coupon platform, to Walmart Labs in 2013, then served as founding CEO of MaGIC, a $21.4M government-backed innovation hub she scaled from zero to 75 employees, launched by President Obama and the Malaysian Prime Minister in 2014. She founded Tiny Health in 2021, motivated by her own child’s struggle with eczema, food sensitivities, and sleep challenges. Donald Koo, Co-Founder & Chief Growth Officer — bringing product engineering expertise from his prior ed-tech company Daily Product Prep (acquired by Exponent in 2022); leads engineering and growth. CMO Dr. Elisa Song MD (integrative pediatrician) and CSO Dr. Kimberley Sukhum PhD (former WashU faculty) lead the clinical and scientific platform.
Lead Investor & Round
The $30M Series B (oversubscribed from an initial $25M target) is led by B Capital Group — a $12B AUM firm founded in 2015 by Eduardo Saverin (Facebook co-founder), Howard Morgan, and Raj Ganguly, with a dedicated $500M Healthcare Fund — anchoring $20M, among the largest consumer healthcare checks the firm has written. Spero Ventures (Series A lead, $35M post-money) is participating pro-rata.
Why Invest in Tiny Health
  • The world’s largest consumer microbiome database: Tiny Health has built the largest paired consumer gut microbiome dataset in the world — 120,000+ fully sequenced profiles with 110,000 matched long-term health records. Johnson & Johnson has expressed willingness to pay $70+ per data point, putting that latent pharmaceutical asset above $8M before any formal commercialization exists.
  • A three-channel flywheel with enterprises as zero-CAC distribution: Tiny Health runs three channels that reinforce each other — direct-to-consumer (67% of 2025 revenue), healthcare providers (6,800+ practitioners, CAC declining from $101 to $44), and enterprise (33% of 2025 revenue, 377% year-over-year growth) that provides essentially zero-cost distribution at scale. All 37 current partnerships were closed inbound; outbound enterprise sales did not start until mid-2026.
  • Peer-reviewed clinical evidence no competitor can claim: A September 2025 peer-reviewed clinical trial showed that the Tiny Health platform works. They demonstrated an 83% reduction in the odds of allergic conditions like eczema in C-section infants who followed the Tiny Health protocol. This published evidence required the same scientific rigor as a drug trial, and funds the deal’s most important optionality — insurance reimbursement, which is not in the current revenue model but pure upside on a business that reaches profitability without a dollar of insurance coverage.
Risks We’re Watching
  • Enterprise revenue concentration: Rupa Health drives ~40% of 2025 enterprise revenue (~$800K of ~$1.9M). The $53M 2026 projection partly depends on converting several pilot partners to active contracts.
  • Subscription retention — a month-12 cliff: Tiny+ shows a 46.3% year-1 renewal rate, below the ~70% benchmark that defines sticky subscription businesses.
  • Regulatory and reimbursement uncertainty: Standard clinical lab certification today, no filed reimbursement application yet; unit economics still rest almost entirely on cash-pay revenue.
