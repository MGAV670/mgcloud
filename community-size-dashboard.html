Definitions & methodology

  • Total — all contacts in the HubSpot database (all contacts view).
  • Unsubscribed — members of the Unsubscribed list (ID 18753).
  • Unengaged — members of the Unengaged list (ID 50265), defined as: has not opened 16 emails in a row if they ever previously opened a marketing email, or has not opened 11 emails in a row if they never opened one. Contacts who are both unsubscribed and unengaged are counted only as Unsubscribed (deduplicated out of Unengaged).
  • Engaged — the remainder: Total − Unsubscribed − Unengaged(net).
