01 — The Goal

Aim for a large, diversified portfolio,
built over many annual vintages

Venture is a power-law asset class. The majority of returns are driven by a minority of your investments. Smart venture investing requires exposure to enough companies, across enough stages, sectors, and years, so that you minimize risk and maximize your ownership in the winners.

Your goal: exposure to hundreds of companies, diversified by stage, sector, and vintage year — built steadily over many years, not a single vintage year.

Hundreds of companies, not a handful

A single startup investment is a long shot. A portfolio of hundreds of companies is an asset class. Scale and access to the right companies is what turns venture from speculation into a strategy.

By stage, sector, and vintage year

No single stage, sector, or vintage year captures the whole picture. Spreading across all three cyclically the natural unevenness of venture cycles and exercises exposure to innovation taking place across the entire economy.
